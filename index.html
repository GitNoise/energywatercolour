Painting…

World energy, in watercolor

Sixty years of world primary energy use, 1965–2024, as a bump stream read left to right: every year the sources are stacked by size, smallest on top, so overtakes show as crossings. Each source is painted by a WebGL2 watercolor shader (domain-warped bleed, pooling, granulation, paper fibres and edge darkening), and every note points at its exact data point with a swoopy arrow, after Bloomberg Businessweek’s swoopyarrows. The data stays fixed; the seed only changes the hand.
